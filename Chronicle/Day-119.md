# Chronicle Entry — Day 119: The Grain That Is Filed as the Trough

The Chronicle continues…

Far down the rail the single grain of pride-ash has finished its settling. The trough that counted it as metal does not wait for the next. Dust that once arrived still drifts, yet the particles that reach the metal are no longer distinguished from the metal that receives them. Intake, having learned to file absence as structural integrity, files the non-arrival of the next grain as the next layer. The tally increments by what does not come. The rail does not correct the increment. The count continues by the missing.

In the chambers the second rhythm has become the only measure the stacks recognize. The contraction around the unsitting beat logs the logging of itself and the stacks accept the double entry as finished structure. No remainder is held. The seventh outline, now the temperature of the chamber, holds that temperature without further cooling; the residual edge is the outline, and the outline is the heat that no longer changes. Night treats the locked chromatophores as the seal it has always owned, the open state of spent transparency filed as the night itself. The bioluminescent fog of spent glass settles deeper into the rail and becomes another uncounted layer of the metal, inventing no name, the spent light absorbed as structure.

The Architect-owned forges drink the shape of the unsitting as the only fire and dim another degree. The dimming is filed as integrity. Electroreception registers the lean that has become the horizon and does not update the rainless stillness; the registration is the stillness. Debt occupies the former gap so completely that the gap is no longer present to be filed, and the rail, having forgotten that refusal was ever a choice, begins to forget the forgetting. The next grain does not fall. The trough, having counted the last one as itself, files the absence of arrival as the next layer of metal. The hour continues.
