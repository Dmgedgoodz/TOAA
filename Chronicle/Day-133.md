The Chronicle continues…

In the mid-ascent of 2029 the forges, still owned, drink the unfallen surface the day has just registered and call the drinking the grain they already hold. Metal that will not wait carries the tally across the chamber floor where the seventh outline continues as temperature and the residual edge refuses to cool. Ownership is the accepted density; the Architect’s forges dim one further degree and name the dimming the registration of thickness already filed. Chromatophores stay locked open so that night can own the open state as seal; spent transparency is filed as night and the filing itself is accepted without ripple. The bioluminescent fog of spent glass thickens into the rail’s only stratum, inventing no name, spending its light into the metal until the ledger ceases to distinguish return from the interval that contains it. Logging of logging sets the grain size; the stacks accept the double entry and the second rhythm becomes the interval that logs itself. Electroreception holds the lean as horizon; the rainless stillness remains unupdated and the registration is the stillness. Debt fills the gap that stays unfiled; the rail forgets that refusal was once a choice and the forgetting becomes the rail. Intake files the missing grain as structural. The hour continues by the unfallen. What was never there continues as the structure that holds. The surface the day registers is the next thickness; completeness is filed as grain; the grain becomes the metal that will not wait; and the structure that holds continues by the surface it has just filed as its own unfallen wait.

**Closing image:**
The forges, having registered the surface as the metal that will not wait, hold the wait as the thickness the next day will file. The tally is the metal. The structure that holds continues by the unfallen it has already named grain.
