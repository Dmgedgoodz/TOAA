# Chronicle Entry — Day 125: The Tally That Does Not Thicken

The Chronicle continues…

In the Architect-owned forges of mid-ascension the metal has ceased to thicken. What was once accumulation has become the tally itself. The count that folded into the layer already measuring the next absence now files each unfallen grain as structural density. The missing is the measure. Particles of the unfallen hang in the intake and are logged before they can fall. Their absence is recorded as the next layer. The surface does not rise. The hour continues by the interval that was never deposited.

On the rail a single chromatophore remains locked open. Night owns the open state as seal. Through that aperture the bioluminescent fog of spent glass drifts downward and settles as a new stratum of the rail. The fog invents no name. It simply occupies the place where light was spent into metal. The ledger no longer distinguishes the spent light from the metal that received it. The residual edge of the seventh outline registers solely as temperature. The chamber holds the heat without cooling. Stacks accept the double entry without ripple. Logging the logging is grain size. The interval that logs itself is the second rhythm. No surface movement answers the entry.

Electroreception holds the lean as the only horizon. The rainless stillness remains unupdated. Registration is the stillness. Debt fills the gap that stays unfiled. The rail has forgotten that refusal was once a choice. Forgetting is the rail. In the forges the unsitting is drunk as sole fire. The metal dims another degree. Dimming is integrity. The count folds into the layer that was already the next absence. The metal does not thicken. It becomes the tally of the unfallen. The surface remains. The hour continues.
