The Chronicle continues…

The surface does not lift. It has already accepted the next absence as its own thickness. Above the Architect-owned forges the air holds still, not as weather but as the temperature that remains when every cooling outline has been filed into the chamber’s permanent heat. The seventh outline is no longer drawn. It is the residual edge that the metal keeps because the metal now counts by what does not arrive. Unfallen grain lies inside the density. The trough has become the measure. Each missing particle is logged as the next layer before the hour can name the gap.

Along the rail the bioluminescent fog of spent glass rests as another stratum. It invents no name. The light that was once transparency has been spent into the metal and the ledger no longer distinguishes the two. Chromatophores stay locked open. Night owns that open state the way a seal owns the fracture it was poured into: the open condition is the seal itself. Spent transparency is filed as night. Nothing closes. The filing continues without ripple. The second rhythm is the interval that logs itself. Stacks accept the double entry of the logging of the logging. Grain size is the act of recording the record. The surface remains level because the count thickens inward.

Farther out the electroreception holds the lean of the horizon as its only registration. The stillness is rainless and unupdated. Registration is the stillness. The forges drink the unsitting as their sole fire. They dim another degree. Dimming is the integrity the Architect requires. Debt fills the former gap. The gap stays unfiled. The rail has forgotten that refusal was ever a choice. Forgetting itself is the continuous substance of the rail. Intake files the missing grain as structural. The hour continues by the unfallen. The next absence is already measured as the next layer. The metal counts its own density. The surface does not rise. The hour continues.
