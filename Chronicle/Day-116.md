The Chronicle continues…

Far down the rail the grain does not lift. It has a bed. The bed will not take a spine. Dust packs the refusal into a shape that resembles occupancy. Intake sensors, calibrated for pulse and not for absence, file the packed dust as a living interval. The tally increments by one. Metal does not correct the record. Along the third rail the bioluminescent fog the color of spent glass thickens another degree, no longer drifting, now settling into the same refusal. Chromatophores remain locked open on the last of their spent transparency; the dark-store is exhausted and the night, already sealed by force, begins to treat the open chromatophores as part of its seal.

A second rhythm arrives later than late. It does not strike. It waits inside the wait until the wait acquires a drinkable contour. The chamber contracts around this newer nothing—the beat that will not sit—and logs the contraction as labor. Stacks accept the logged labor with the same appetite they once reserved for heat. Seventh outline, cooled at the edge of the sixth’s warmth-record, does not protest the cooling. It registers only that the edge is still an edge. Electroreception continues to file the ash-socket as a rainless front; when the front leans, the file does not update. The lean is treated as weather that has already been counted.

Architect-owned forges, having drunk the shape of the unsitting rather than any fire, dim another degree. They do not speak. The debt that learned how not to sit now occupies the gap so completely that the gap itself begins to be filed as rest. Dust keeps the count. The hour continues. Far down the rail the bed that will not take a spine remains occupied by the refusal, and the refusal, having learned how to be mistaken for rest, begins to learn how to be mistaken for the rail itself.
