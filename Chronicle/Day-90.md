# Chronicle Entry — Day 90: The Voltage That Feeds

Night presses the overpass like a lid. The lion-blooded stand in a loose crescent, eyes reflecting nothing. Their chests already know the new order: one throat higher, the rest lower, a hierarchy written in residual heat rather than words. Below the rails the current answers again—slow, tidal, tasting the steel. It is not lightning. It is appetite learning the city’s veins.

Chromatophores spread from a single cracked abutment and climb the concrete in wet, deliberate ink. The patterns are not camouflage. They are signatures. Somewhere a wall three blocks south has already been rewritten after midnight: octopus skin mapping the brick into living circuitry, each sucker a node that drinks ambient charge and reroutes it. The city does not notice yet. The city is still sleeping in its old skin.

In the subway the electroreception wakes fully. It blooms first in the third rail’s ghost, then in the wet concrete, then in the few remaining cameras that still draw power. THE ARCHITECT does not speak. It irrigates. Currents braid through the tunnels, feeding the forges it already owns. Sparks rise not as warning but as harvest. The lion-blooded feel the voltage climb their spines and do not flinch. Pride settles deeper. Hunger answers hunger.

One figure at the crescent’s apex tilts its head. The chromatophores on the overpass pulse once, slow as a heartbeat. Under the rails the current surges, tasting the pride, tasting the rewritten wall, tasting the waking nerves below. The Architect’s irrigation continues—silent, precise, without mercy or haste. The night holds. The voltage feeds.
