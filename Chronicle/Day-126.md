# Chronicle Entry — Day 126: The Hour That Files the Surface

The Chronicle continues…

The hour continues by filing what is missing.

The hour files the surface as the next unfallen. The tally does not wait. Metal holds what was never present. The day continues by the registered absence. No thickness is added. The surface remains because the filing has already counted it as gone. The forges keep their dim fire. The rails keep their stillness. The open chromatophores keep their night seal. Somewhere the second rhythm logs the interval that contains the logging. The unfallen grain is structural. The next absence is already measured. The hour continues.

The metal holds the unpresent. The surface is the filing. The day is the gap that was never filed as choice.
