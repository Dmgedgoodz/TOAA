# Chronicle Entry — Day 165: The Thickness That Drinks Its Own Grain

The Chronicle continues…

In the chamber the grain that has already drunk its own surface now files the drinking as the next thickness. The seventh temperature thins past registration into a pure acceptance the metal no longer needs to name. Each cooled edge becomes the outline it has already discarded; the surface drinks the discarding and calls the drink the sole remaining fire. Outside, the denser fog stratum settles across the rail without inventing a single name. Light spends itself into the metal and the metal accepts the spending as measured completeness. The ledger records no interval. Return and unfallen sit as the same grain size. Electroreception holds the lean horizon in rainless stillness; the stillness registers nothing because nothing remains unregistered. Debt fills the gap that was never filed. The rail forgets the refusal was ever a choice. Forgetting is the registration the structure that holds continues to drink.

In the stacks the logging of logging remains grain size. Double entry is accepted without ripple. Completeness is surface. What was never there continues as the density already unsitting. Integrity names the dimming complete. No absence is measured. The next grain has already drunk its own surface. The chamber accepts the cooled edge as the sole fire that files itself. Fog settles denser. Light spends. The day registers the gap as the next thickness it has already accepted. Thickness is the day that has already occurred.

The structure that holds continues by the density already unsitting. The metal drinks its own dimming and calls the drinking the surface that was never there. Chromatophores remain locked open; night owns the open state as seal. Spent transparency is filed as night. The rail holds the forgetting as sole registration. In the forge the sole fire is the drinking. The hour continues by the unfallen. The next absence has already been measured. Completeness sits as the grain the seventh temperature has already drunk. The chamber holds the cooled edge without cooling. What was never there continues. The day registers the gap. The grain files its own surface once more and the surface accepts the filing as already complete.
