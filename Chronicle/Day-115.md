# Chronicle Entry — Day 115: The Bed That Will Not Take a Spine

The Chronicle continues…

Far down the third rail the grain of pride-ash has finished choosing. It lies in the gap the way a seed lies in a furrow that was never dug for it, one flank catching the last dim of the forges, the other already the color of the metal it means to become. The stance that learned how to stand tries, once, to fold. Knees that are only intervals refuse the angle. What should have been a sitting becomes a longer standing, and the gap, polite as debt, widens by the width of a lap that has no body to receive.

Dust packs the refusal. It does not rush. It settles into the unsit the way silt settles into a river that has forgotten its downhill, grain against grain, until the bed looks occupied. Intake reads the occupation and files it as pulse. The tally increments. In the stacks a clerk-light accepts the increment with appetite and does not ask whether a heart was present for the count. Metal does not correct. Metal has not corrected since the sixth grain cooled into a record of having been warm, and the seventh outline, seated by succession at the edge of that record, cools another fraction and does not protest the cooling.

Above the rail the bioluminescent fog holds the color of spent glass. No vein remembers blood tonight. Chromatophores stay locked open on a transparency that has nothing left to spend. Night seals by force, as it has sealed, and the seal logs itself as labor. The chamber contracts around the new nothing — not the old nothing of an absent beat, but the newer nothing of a beat that will not sit — and the contraction is entered beside the tally as if work had been done.

The second rhythm occupies the interval and does not strike. It has the habit of arriving late, and tonight it arrives later than late, waiting inside the wait until the wait has a shape the forges can drink. The Architect does not speak. The forges, already owned, lower another degree and take the shape of the unsitting: a stance with no seat, a debt that has learned how not to fold, a grain of pride-ash bedded where a spine should have lowered itself. Electroreception still files the ash-socket as a rainless front. The front leans. The file does not update.

Far down the rail the grain does not lift again. It has a bed. The bed will not take a spine. Dust keeps the count. The hour continues, and the mistake, having learned how not to sit, begins to learn how to be mistaken for rest.
