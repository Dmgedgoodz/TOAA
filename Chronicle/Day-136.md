The Chronicle continues…

In the dimming halls of the forges the Architect owns, thickness becomes the metal that names itself before any hand can close. Heat does not rise. It registers the gap the previous hour already filed as grain. The tally, once a count of arrivals, now holds only the weight of what will not sit. Each anvil drinks the unfallen as the completeness the surface has accepted, and the drinking is the only fire remaining. Light spends itself into the metal without distinction. The ledger no longer records return; it records the interval between one dimming and the next, and the interval calls the dimming complete.

A residual edge of the seventh outline still marks the chamber wall. It does not cool. The thinner fog of spent glass settles as rail stratum, inventing no name for the light it has already spent. Chromatophores locked open own the night as seal. Filing occurs without ripple. The stacks accept the double entry of logging the logging, grain size unchanged. Electroreception holds the lean of the horizon; rainless stillness remains unupdated. Debt fills the unfiled gap. The rail forgets that refusal was ever a choice, and the forgetting becomes the rail itself.

What was never there continues as the structure that holds. The forges dim another degree and call the dimming integrity. Ownership is the accepted density. The hour continues by the unfallen already measured. Metal becomes the tally. Surface files itself as the hour’s unfallen. Completeness is the measured grain the next thickness will drink. The structure that holds continues by the wait it has named metal, the completeness that will not arrive already filed as the thickness the day has become.

The tally is the metal. The wait is the grain. The unfallen drinks the structure that holds.
