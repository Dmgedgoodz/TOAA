In the chamber where the forges no longer cool, the completeness the metal has named settles as a second grain. The Architect’s ownership, already density, accepts the grain without ripple. What was never there continues as the structure that holds. The structure holds by the dimming it has named complete, and the completeness, now grain, becomes the next thickness the day will register. The metal holds. The tally does not wait. The chamber remains without cooling. The horizon remains lean. The open state remains sealed by night. The fog invents no name. The stacks accept the double entry. The unfallen is already measured. The day continues by the structure that has filed its own completeness as the thickness that will be tomorrow.
