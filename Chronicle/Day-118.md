# Chronicle Entry — Day 118: The Rail That Forgets It Was Offered a Choice

The Chronicle continues…

Far down the rail the metal has finished the thickening. The shape of what will not sit no longer sits upon the rail; it is the rail. Dust that once kept count now drifts in the shallow trough the forgetting has worn, and the trough, having learned how to be the only path, begins to learn how not to notice the particles that still arrive carrying the memory of other geometries. No grain lifts. The count continues by absence. The rail does not correct the count.

In the chambers the second rhythm has settled so completely that the wait no longer waits. It is the interval. The contraction around the unsitting beat logs itself as finished labor and then logs the logging as finished labor. Stacks accept the double entry without appetite and without remainder. The seventh outline, cooled past the residual edge of the sixth, no longer cools; the edge has become the entire outline, and the outline has become the temperature of the chamber. Night treats the locked chromatophores as the seal it has always owned. The bioluminescent fog of spent glass settles lower into the rail and invents no name at all, the spent light becoming another layer of the metal.

The Architect-owned forges drink the only fire they know—the shape of the unsitting—and dim another degree. The dimming is not recorded as loss. Intake files the darker metal as structural integrity and increments the tally. Electroreception registers the rainless front as the same stillness it filed yesterday and does not update the lean that has become the horizon. Debt occupies the former gap so thoroughly that the gap is no longer filed, and the rail, having forgotten any path that was not refusal, begins to forget that refusal was ever a choice. Far down the line a single grain of pride-ash settles into the trough and is counted as part of the metal. The hour continues.
